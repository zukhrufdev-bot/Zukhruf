Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple one-page or small multi-page site usually takes 1 to 2 weeks. Larger sites with extra features can take 3 to 4 weeks. After we talk, I'll give you a clear timeline before I start.

How much do you charge?

Every project is different, so I price each one based on the number of pages, features, and design work involved. Send me a message through the contact form and I'll reply with a clear quote, with no hidden costs.

Do I need to have my content and images ready?

It helps, but it's not required. If you share your ideas, logo, and any photos you have, I'll organise everything into a clean layout. If you're missing something, I'll guide you on what to prepare.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website I build is fully responsive, so it looks good and works smoothly on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Can I request changes while the site is being built?

Of course. I share progress with you along the way, and each project includes a set number of revision rounds so the final result matches your vision. We'll agree on this before we begin.

How does payment work?

I ask for an advance payment to begin the work, and the remaining balance is due once you're happy with the finished site, before it goes live.

Will you help after the website is launched?

Yes. I'm available for small fixes right after launch, and I can also handle future updates or new pages whenever you need them.

How do we get started?

Just fill out the contact form with a few details about your project. I'll get back to you to discuss your goals and next steps.
