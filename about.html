About Zukhruf

Assalamualaikum! I’m the developer behind Zukhruf. My journey into web development began with a question I couldn’t stop asking: how does this actually work?

I came from engineering and medicine, fields built on precision, but every website I opened made me curious about what was happening behind the screen. That curiosity pulled me into code, and what started as late nights of trial, error, and debugging slowly became a craft I love.

I chose the name Zukhruf because it means adornment, the beauty that makes something feel finished and cared for. That is how I see every website: it should work well, and it should also feel like it truly belongs to the person it was made for.

My other fields taught me to be patient and careful with details, and I bring that into every site I build. I work alone, so you always deal directly with the person designing and writing your code. My style is handmade: warm colors, soft details, and pages that feel personal instead of copied from a template.

Whether you’re a small business owner getting your first website or someone with an idea that needs the right execution, I give every project the same attention, because your website isn’t just a product to me, it’s a reflection of what you’re building.

I’m still growing, and I’m honest about that. This is just the beginning of Zukhruf, and I’m excited for who I get to build it with next. In sha Allah!

Zukhruf

Small details. Clear websites.
